Documentation: this page and its API; tet itself: the mathematics, the methods, their limits and the credits; the syntax of the arguments.

API: /?prec=20&func=tet&base=2&height=0.5, /?func=ate&base=2&value=65536, /?func=ssrt&value=16&height=3, or with four real numbers /?func=tet&base_re=1.5&base_im=0.5&height_re=1&height_im=0.7. The reply is JSON; &format=text gives the two lines that tet prints. In such a URL a + is a plus sign and a space is %20.

Results are computed by tet on this server, to as many digits as are asked for, and kept in memory until the server restarts. A calculation that is not ready after {{wait}} continues there, for up to {{limit}}: this page then asks again by itself, and the same request made later, here or to the API, finds the calculation running, or its result. At most {{jobs}} calculations run at a time: while all of them are busy a new one is turned away, to be asked for again later. Source: claude-tetration (AGPL-3.0){{revision}}.
